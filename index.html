Project Stories

The story behind each project

Mobile app for retail branch managers

Retail · Mobile app · Sole business analyst

The story: a large retail brand needed a mobile app for the people who run its branches, so managers could do their daily work in one place.

What I did: I was the business analyst and owned all of the analysis. I met branch managers and stakeholders to understand how branches really work and what slowed them down. I turned those needs into business requirements, process flows, user stories and acceptance criteria that the team could build from. I answered the developers' questions, kept the requirements up to date as things changed, then supported UAT, validated defects and checked the app against what the business asked for.

Requirements gatheringProcess flowsUser storiesAcceptance criteriaUAT supportDefect validation

Online certificate system for a government department

Government · Web system · Requirements and process documentation

The story: a government education department's online certificate system was due for a revamp.

What I did: I studied how the existing system and its processes worked and found where users and staff struggled. I worked with stakeholders to decide what the new version needed to do, then documented the current-state and future-state processes and wrote the requirements behind the improved system, so the build team had a clear, agreed specification.

Current-state analysisFuture-state processBRD and requirementsStakeholder workshops

Point-of-sale and mini ERP for a retail clothing brand

Retail · POS and ERP · Project coordination and BA support

The story: a retail clothing brand needed a point-of-sale system and a mini ERP, from the shop counter to the back office.

What I did: As project coordinator I kept the client, developers and QA moving together. I captured and documented requirements, tracked progress and reported status, raised risks and issues early, and supported testing until the system was ready for the business.
